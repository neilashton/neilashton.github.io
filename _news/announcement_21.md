[FluidsBench](https://fluidsbench.org/) is now live, providing an open framework for evaluating AI surrogate models in fluid dynamics. Submissions are open for five datasets, with the first official leaderboard planned for 5 December 2026. [Evaluate and submit your model](https://fluidsbench.org/run/) by 5 December, 23:59:59 AoE (Anywhere on Earth, UTC−12), for consideration in the first release.
