This website does not load Google Analytics unless you choose **Allow analytics**. If you allow it, anonymised usage information helps measure visits and outbound links to services such as YouTube, Spotify and Apple Podcasts. Google Analytics may then set first-party measurement cookies. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google Signals are disabled.

Your analytics choice is saved in your browser's local storage. You can review or change it at any time using the **Analytics settings** control in the footer. Choosing **No thanks** prevents analytics from loading on future page views in that browser.

Embedded YouTube videos use YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain. Spotify is contacted only after you choose to load its player. Opening links to external services is governed by those services' own privacy policies.

For questions about this website, email [contact@neilashton.co.uk](mailto:contact@neilashton.co.uk).
