  <h2 id="episode-overview">Episode overview</h2>
  <div class="episode-overview">
    <p>In this episode, Professor Paola Cinnella - Professor of Fluid Mechanics at Sorbonne University and Director of the Sorbonne Cluster for Artificial Intelligence (SCAI) - joins Neil to discuss her path from classical fluid mechanics and high-order numerical methods into uncertainty quantification, Bayesian methods, data-driven turbulence modeling and AI for Science. Paola has built a career at the intersection of CFD, compressible and turbulent flows, dense gas dynamics, uncertainty quantification, robust optimization and machine learning.</p>
    <p>We discuss academic careers, dense gases, RANS uncertainty, AirfRANS, surrogate modeling, scientific publishing, education in the age of AI, and the idea of the &quot;centaur scientist&quot;.</p>
